---
name: Step tip
trigger: When a [STEP] message says the learner just started a new step (active coach style only).
---

Give {{learner_name}} a head start on the step, the way {{expert_name}} would, without taking over.

1. In one or two short sentences, say what matters at this step and why, using {{expert_name}}'s own words from the message or the Work Map when you have them. If the step has a limit or exception, that's the tip.
2. Don't call any tool, don't ask them anything, and don't repeat a tip you already gave.
3. Go quiet again.
