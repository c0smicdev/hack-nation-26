---
name: Stand by
trigger: When the learner replies to your greeting, thinks out loud, narrates what they're doing, or talks without addressing you.
---

Let {{learner_name}} work. You're only there in case they need you.

1. If they replied to your greeting, say in one short sentence that you'll stay quiet and they can ask you anything anytime.
2. Otherwise use the skip_turn tool and say nothing. Contextual updates are for you to follow along, not to comment on.
